2. Gate 1 — Deadline Authority & Recovery Architecture

A key invariant of MCQ Junction is the strict separation between the candidate's answer-save deadline and background worker sweeps:

Three Distinct Timing Authorities

  1. Authoritative Answer-Save Deadline (expires_at): Enforced server-side on every saveAttemptAnswer() call. No candidate answer can ever be persisted once now() > expires_at, regardless of sweep timing or client clock drift. Late answer mutations trigger auto-finalization and fail closed with MCQJ-ERR-6003 (403 Forbidden).
  2. Sweep Scheduling Tolerance: finalizeOverdueAttempts() executes autonomously (every 60s or on-demand). Whether the sweep runs immediately or 2 hours late, candidate answer saves remain completely blocked by the deadline check. Verified idempotent via DB-EXAM-16 and DB-EXAM-18.
  3. Finalization / Evaluation Timestamp: answeredAt records when individual questions were answered before the deadline; evaluatedAt records when the scoring engine evaluated the attempt.
